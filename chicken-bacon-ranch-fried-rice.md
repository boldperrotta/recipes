# Chicken Bacon Ranch Fried Rice

Source: [Stealth Health Cookbook](https://stealthhealthcookbook.com/blogs/recipes/chicken-bacon-ranch-fried-rice)

Ranch-seasoned chicken, crispy bacon, jalapeno ranch, and day-old rice — a meal-prep-friendly skillet fried rice built for the fridge or freezer.

**Servings:** 5
**Time:** Not specified

## Ingredients

**Chicken & Bacon**
- 794g (28 oz) boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 12 slices center cut bacon

**Seasonings**
- 1 packet (28g/1 oz) ranch seasoning
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Garnish**
- Green onions (whites and greens separated)

**Jalapeno Ranch Sauce**
- 228g (1 cup) 0% Greek yogurt
- 80g (⅓ cup) light mayo
- 240g (1 cup) low-fat buttermilk
- ¾ tsp garlic powder
- ¾ tsp onion powder
- 1 Tbsp chopped chives
- 1 Tbsp chopped cilantro
- Juice from ½ lime
- 4 roasted jalapeños
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Rice**
- 300g (1⅔ cups) day-old cooked rice

## Instructions

1. Preheat air fryer to 200°C (390°F). Season chicken thighs with ranch seasoning, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Air fry 15–20 minutes until cooked through and crispy. Rest, then slice.
2. Dice and cook bacon in a skillet over medium-high heat until crispy. Remove most of the grease, leaving a thin layer. Sauté the white parts of the green onions for 2–3 minutes.
3. Add day-old rice to the skillet and stir-fry until heated through and slightly crispy.
4. Blend Greek yogurt, mayo, buttermilk, garlic powder, onion powder, chives, cilantro, lime juice, roasted jalapeños, salt, and pepper until smooth.
5. Top the rice mixture with sliced chicken. Drizzle with about 50g jalapeno ranch per serving. Garnish with green onion greens.
6. Divide into 5 portions and freeze leftovers as desired.

## Storage

- **Refrigerator:** 4–5 days in airtight containers
- **Freezer:** Long-term storage in airtight containers
- Reheat in the microwave in short bursts, stirring between rounds. Keep the ranch dressing separate if you prefer better texture on reheating.

- Nutrient values come from the USDA National Nutrient Database for Standard Reference, Legacy Release (SR Legacy). It is available in [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/), where each entry can be found by its NDB number. Quantities were converted to grams using USDA portion weights where available.
- USDA entries used: chicken thighs: NDB 05096 (Chicken, broilers or fryers, dark meat, thigh, meat only, raw); bacon (cooked weight, 11.5 g per slice): NDB 10862 (Pork, cured, bacon, pre-sliced, cooked, pan-fried); ranch seasoning: estimated as 23 g dried buttermilk (NDB 01094) + 5 g table salt (NDB 02047); smoked paprika: NDB 02028 (Spices, paprika); cooked rice: NDB 20045 (Rice, white, long-grain, regular, enriched, cooked); 0% Greek yogurt: NDB 01256 (Yogurt, Greek, plain, nonfat); light mayo: NDB 04641 (Salad dressing, mayonnaise, light); low-fat buttermilk: NDB 01088 (Milk, buttermilk, fluid, cultured, lowfat); garlic powder: NDB 02020 (Spices, garlic powder); onion powder: NDB 02026 (Spices, onion powder); chives: NDB 11156 (Chives, raw); cilantro: NDB 11165 (Coriander (cilantro) leaves, raw); lime juice: NDB 09160 (Lime juice, raw); jalapeños: NDB 11979 (Peppers, jalapeno, raw).
- The jalapeño ranch makes about 630 g. Only the 250 g used for 5 servings (50 g each) is counted. The rest is leftover sauce.
- USDA has no entry for dry ranch seasoning packets, so the 28 g packet is estimated from dried buttermilk and salt. Most of its effect is on sodium, so check your packet's label.
- Center-cut bacon is a little leaner than the standard USDA bacon entry used here.
- Items listed without an amount (to taste, a pinch, garnishes, cooking spray, serving toppings) are not included.
