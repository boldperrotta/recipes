# White Chocolate Ice Cream

A smooth and creamy white chocolate ice cream from David Lebovitz.

Source: Eva Bakes, adapted from *The Perfect Scoop* by David Lebovitz

**Servings:** 1 quart (about 8 half-cup servings)
**Prep time:** 10 minutes
**Cook time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 35 minutes, plus chilling (preferably overnight) and churning

## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup whole milk
- 2 cups heavy cream
- Pinch of salt
- 8 oz white chocolate, finely chopped (Eva used two Ghirardelli bars, broken into pieces)
- 5 large egg yolks

## Instructions

1. In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine the sugar, milk, and salt and warm the mixture.
2. Place the chopped white chocolate in a large bowl and set a fine mesh strainer over the bowl.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg yolks. Gradually pour in some of the warm milk mixture, whisking constantly, to temper the yolks. Then pour the warmed yolks back into the saucepan.
4. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until the custard is thick enough to coat a spatula or the back of a wooden spoon.
5. Strain the custard into the white chocolate and stir until the chocolate is completely melted.
6. Stir in the heavy cream, then chill thoroughly in the refrigerator, preferably overnight.
7. Once the mixture is completely cold, freeze it in your ice cream maker according to the manufacturer's instructions.
