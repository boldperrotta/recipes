# Guinness, Whiskey & Irish Cream Cupcakes

Guinness chocolate cupcakes with an Irish whiskey ganache center, topped with Baileys Irish Cream frosting.

Source: [Brown Eyed Baker](https://www.browneyedbaker.com/irish-car-bomb-cupcakes/) (Michelle Lettrich)

**Servings:** 24 cupcakes
**Prep time:** 40 minutes
**Cook time:** 17 minutes
**Total time:** 1 hour, plus cooling

## Ingredients

**Cupcakes:**
- 1 cup Guinness stout
- 1 cup unsalted butter, at room temperature
- ¾ cup Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 1½ tsp baking soda
- ¾ tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- ⅔ cup sour cream

**Whiskey ganache filling:**
- 8 oz bittersweet chocolate, finely chopped
- ⅔ cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp butter, at room temperature
- 2 tsp Irish whiskey (such as Jameson)

**Baileys frosting:**
- 2 cups unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 5 cups powdered sugar
- 6 tbsp Baileys Irish Cream

## Instructions

**Make the cupcakes:**

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line two standard muffin tins with liners.
2. Bring the Guinness and butter to a simmer in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the cocoa powder and whisk until smooth. Remove from the heat and let cool slightly.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking soda, and salt; set aside.
4. With an electric mixer, beat the eggs and sour cream on medium speed until combined. Add the Guinness-chocolate mixture and beat just to combine.
5. Reduce the speed to low, add the flour mixture, and beat just until it starts to come together, about 30 seconds. Fold with a rubber spatula until completely combined.
6. Divide the batter among the liners. Bake until a thin knife inserted into the center comes out clean, about 17 minutes.
7. Cool for 5 minutes in the pan, then move the cupcakes to a wire rack to cool completely.

**Make the whiskey ganache:**

8. Place the chocolate in a heatproof bowl. Bring the heavy cream to a simmer in a small saucepan over medium heat, then immediately pour it over the chocolate and let sit for 2 minutes.
9. With a rubber spatula, gently stir from the center outward until smooth. Stir in the butter and whiskey.
10. Let the ganache cool until thick but still soft enough to pipe, about 30 minutes. If it gets too stiff, give it a good whisk to loosen it.

**Fill the cupcakes:**

11. With a paring knife, cut the centers out of the cooled cupcakes, going about two-thirds of the way down.
12. With a cookie scoop or spoon, divide the ganache among the centers of the cupcakes.

**Make the Baileys frosting:**

13. With the whisk attachment of a stand mixer, whip the butter on medium-high speed for 5 minutes, scraping down the bowl occasionally.
14. Reduce the speed to medium-low and gradually add the powdered sugar until fully incorporated.
15. Add the Baileys, increase the speed to medium-high, and whip for another 2–3 minutes, until light and fluffy.

**Finish:**

16. Frost the cupcakes with a piping tip or an offset spatula, and decorate with sprinkles if desired.

## Notes

- Vegetarian. Contains alcohol: the whiskey in the ganache and the Baileys in the frosting are not cooked off.
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 4 days.
- Piping tip: the source decorated them with a Wilton 1M open star tip, and later with a slightly larger Ateco #827 open star tip.
