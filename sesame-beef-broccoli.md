# Sesame Beef Broccoli

Source: [Fit Men Cook](https://fitmencook.com/recipes/sesame-beef-broccoli/)

A quick Asian-inspired stir-fry, about 30 minutes total including prep.

**Servings:** 4
**Time:** ~30 minutes

## Ingredients

**Protein & Vegetables**
- 1.5 lb lean flank steak, thinly sliced
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced

**Sauce**
- 1/2 cup low sodium beef stock
- 1/2 cup Bragg Liquid Aminos (or low sodium soy sauce)
- 1 cup blackberries (optional)
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 5 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon ginger
- 2 tablespoons arrowroot starch
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 3 tablespoons raw honey (optional)

**Garnish**
- Green onions
- Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Whisk together beef stock, liquid aminos, ginger, sesame oil, arrowroot starch, and rice vinegar in a bowl.
2. Heat a saucepan over medium-high with olive oil spray. Add garlic and blackberries; sauté 6-8 minutes until the berries break down, stirring to prevent burning.
3. Pour the sauce mixture into the skillet, reduce heat to medium, and stir until thickened. Add honey if desired. Set aside.
4. Heat a wok or large skillet over medium-high. Cook the beef slices for about 4 minutes, until mostly no longer pink.
5. Add the bell peppers and stir quickly for about 3 minutes, then add the broccoli.
6. Cook the beef and broccoli together for 5 minutes while stirring. Reduce heat to medium and pour in the sauce. Toss to coat evenly and cook 2-3 minutes more.
7. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds. Serve with brown rice or quinoa.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 392
- Protein: 40g
- Fat: 14g
- Carbohydrates: 26g
- Fiber: 3g
- Sugar: 17g
