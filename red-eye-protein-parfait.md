# Red Eye Protein Parfait

A delicious blend of coffee, protein, and wholesome ingredients — a breakfast with a caffeine kick and a big hit of protein.

Source: [Fit Men Cook](https://fitmencook.com/recipes/red-eye-protein-parfait/)

**Servings:** 1

## Ingredients

- 1 scoop lean whey isolate protein powder
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon cacao powder
- 1 tablespoon instant coffee
- ½ tablespoon organic honey
- ⅓ cup granola
- ¼ cup blueberries

## Instructions

1. Combine half the yogurt with the protein powder, cacao powder, instant coffee, and honey in a bowl.
2. Mix until smooth.
3. Layer the mixture in a glass jar or bowl, then top with the remaining yogurt, granola, and blueberries.

Note: Layering is optional but enhances visual appeal. Raw cacao powder offers more antioxidants than chocolate protein powder.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 521
- Protein: 53g
- Fat: 10g
- Carbohydrates: 55g
