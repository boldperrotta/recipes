# Meal Prep Breakfast Burritos

Freezer-friendly breakfast burritos loaded with roasted potatoes, sausage, peppers, onions, eggs, and cheddar — perfect for grab-and-go breakfasts throughout the week or month.

Source: [Kay Nutrition](https://kaynutrition.com/meal-prep-breakfast-burritos/)

**Yield:** 8 burritos
**Prep time:** 20 minutes
**Cook time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 pound white potatoes, cubed (½-inch)
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon black pepper
- 1 pound pork or turkey sausage (casings removed)
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, seeded and diced
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 12 eggs
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 8 flour tortillas (10-inch)

**Optional toppings:** salsa, sour cream, avocado

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Toss potatoes with 2 tablespoons olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper; roast 20–25 minutes until golden.
3. Whisk eggs in a large bowl; set aside.
4. Heat remaining olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat; cook sausage 5–8 minutes, breaking into pieces, then transfer to a plate.
5. In the same skillet, cook onion and bell pepper 3–4 minutes; transfer to the plate with the sausage.
6. Melt butter in the skillet over low-medium heat; add eggs and scramble 2–4 minutes; transfer to a plate.
7. Lay tortillas on a work surface and line the center of each with potatoes, sausage, vegetables, eggs, and cheese.
8. Fold the left and right sides inward, fold the bottom edge up, and roll upward until closed.
9. *Optional:* sear each burrito seam-side down 1–2 minutes per side until golden.
10. Wrap individually in parchment paper and aluminum foil; store in an airtight container.
11. Refrigerate up to 4 days or freeze up to 3 months.

## Reheating

- **Refrigerated:** oven/toaster oven at 400°F for 10–15 minutes, or microwave for 2–4 minutes.
- **Frozen:** oven/toaster oven at 400°F for 25–30 minutes, or microwave for 5–7 minutes.
