# Healthy White Chicken Chili

A 30-minute chili made with tender chicken, white beans, corn, and green chilies in a creamy broth — thickened with Greek yogurt instead of heavy cream. Works as a stovetop, slow cooker, or Instant Pot recipe.

Source: [iFOODreal](https://ifoodreal.com/healthy-white-chicken-chili/)

**Servings:** 6
**Prep time:** 5 minutes
**Cook time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 large garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon oil (avocado oil)
- 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 cans (15 oz each) great Northern or cannellini beans, drained
- 2 cups corn
- 1 can (4 oz) diced green chilies
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- ½ teaspoon chili powder
- ⅛ teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 small bunch cilantro, chopped
- ½ cup Greek yogurt (2%+ fat)
- Juice of 1 lime
- Tortilla chips, avocado, lime, for serving

## Instructions

### Stovetop

1. Heat a Dutch oven over medium-high heat; add oil. Sauté onion and garlic for 3 minutes.
2. Add cumin, oregano, chili powder, cayenne, salt, and pepper; cook 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
3. Mash about 1 cup of beans in a bowl with a fork.
4. Add broth, whole beans, mashed beans, corn, green chilies, and raw chicken to the pot. Cover, bring to a boil, then simmer on low-medium heat for 20 minutes.
5. Remove from heat. Shred chicken with forks or meat claws, then return it to the pot.
6. Stir in lime juice, cilantro, and yogurt.
7. Serve warm with desired toppings.

### Slow Cooker

1. Sauté onion, garlic, and spices in a skillet for 3 minutes; transfer to the slow cooker.
2. Mash 1 cup of beans; add to the slow cooker with only 1 cup of broth, the whole beans, corn, green chilies, and chicken.
3. Cover and cook on Low for 6 hours or High for 3 hours.
4. Shred chicken and return it to the pot; stir in cilantro, lime juice, and yogurt.

### Instant Pot

1. Mash about 1 cup of beans.
2. Add ingredients in this order (do not stir): garlic, onion, corn, whole and mashed beans, chicken, green chilies, cumin, oregano, chili powder, cayenne, salt, pepper, and only 1 cup of broth.
3. Close the lid, set valve to Sealing, and pressure cook on High/Manual for 20 minutes.
4. Quick release immediately after cooking.
5. Shred chicken; stir in cilantro, lime juice, and yogurt.

## Storage

- Refrigerate up to 5 days in an airtight container.
- Freeze up to 3 months in an airtight container.
- Reheat from thawed on the stovetop, slow cooker, microwave, or Instant Pot steam function.
