# Mexican Beef and Rice Casserole

A hearty one-dish Mexican-inspired casserole with seasoned ground beef, spinach, corn, tomatoes, rice, and melted cheese, baked all in one pan.

Source: [Jo Cooks](https://www.jocooks.com/recipes/mexican-beef-and-rice-casserole)

**Servings:** 8
**Prep time:** 10 minutes
**Cook time:** 1 hour
**Total time:** 1 hour 10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 tablespoon taco seasoning
- 1 bunch spinach, ends trimmed, roughly chopped
- 1 cup frozen corn kernels
- 14 oz fire-roasted diced tomatoes (1 can)
- ½ cup salsa
- 2 cups Mexican 4-cheese blend, shredded, divided
- 1 cup long-grain rice, uncooked
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Spray a 9×13-inch casserole dish with cooking spray.
2. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook ground beef for about 5 minutes, breaking it into crumbles. Stir in taco seasoning and cook until browned and no longer pink.
3. Add spinach, corn, tomatoes, salsa, and half the cheese to the skillet. Mix well, then add the uncooked rice and broth, stirring until evenly combined.
4. Transfer the mixture to the prepared dish, cover with foil, and bake for 45 minutes, until the rice is tender.
5. Remove the foil, top with the remaining cheese, and bake an additional 5 minutes until melted.
6. Garnish with cilantro and serve immediately.

## Tips

- Brown the meat well to develop flavor before adding the vegetables.
- Keep the dish covered initially to create steam for proper rice cooking.
- Let the casserole rest briefly after baking to help the flavors meld.
- Can be assembled ahead and refrigerated before baking (add extra cooking time).
- Freezes well for up to 2–3 months.
