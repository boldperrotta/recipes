# Caribbean-Inspired Chicken & Plantain Curry Meal Prep

Source: [Fit Men Cook](https://fitmencook.com/recipes/caribbean-chicken-curry/)

A high-protein, high-carb curry meal prep with seasoned chicken, seared plantains, and coconut curry sauce over cauliflower rice. Good post-workout meal.

**Servings:** 4
**Time:** 20 min prep, 20 min cook (~40 minutes total)

## Ingredients

**Seasoned Cauliflower Rice**
- 1½ large heads cauliflower, riced into florets
- 1 teaspoon bouillon seasoning
- 2 teaspoons dried thyme

**Plantains**
- 2 tablespoons avocado oil
- 2 medium plantains, peeled and sliced on the bias

**Chicken Marinade**
- 1½ lb chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 green onion stalks, chopped (green parts only)
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon cayenne
- Sea salt and pepper, pinch

**Curry Sauce**
- 1½ tablespoons curry powder
- 2 cups lite canned coconut milk (about 1.5 cans)
- Sea salt and pepper, to taste

**Garnish**
- Red pepper
- Red onion
- Fresh cilantro or parsley

## Instructions

1. Combine chicken with the marinade seasonings and oil in a bowl. Toss, cover, and marinate 20 minutes.
2. Prepare the cauliflower rice, then mix in the bouillon seasoning and thyme. Set aside.
3. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add avocado oil and plantain slices; sear undisturbed about 3 minutes per side until marked. Remove from skillet.
4. Cook the chicken in the same skillet until seared, 4-6 minutes (doesn't need to be fully cooked through yet). Return the plantains to the skillet, sprinkle in the curry powder, and gently fold together.
5. Reduce heat to medium and pour in the coconut milk. Bring to a simmer, then reduce to low, cover, and cook 12-15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through. Remove from heat and let the sauce thicken. Optional: stir in 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water for a creamier sauce.
6. Divide the cauliflower rice among containers, top with the curry mixture, and garnish with red pepper, red onion, and cilantro or parsley. Refrigerate up to 4-5 days, or freeze unused portions within 2 days.

- Protein: 46g
