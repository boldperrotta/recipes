# Red Eye Protein Parfait

A delicious blend of coffee, protein, and wholesome ingredients — a breakfast with a caffeine kick and a big hit of protein.

Source: [Fit Men Cook](https://fitmencook.com/recipes/red-eye-protein-parfait/)

**Servings:** 1

## Ingredients

- 1 scoop lean whey isolate protein powder
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon cacao powder
- 1 tablespoon instant coffee
- ½ tablespoon organic honey
- ⅓ cup granola
- ¼ cup blueberries

## Instructions

1. Combine half the yogurt with the protein powder, cacao powder, instant coffee, and honey in a bowl.
2. Mix until smooth.
3. Layer the mixture in a glass jar or bowl, then top with the remaining yogurt, granola, and blueberries.

Note: Layering is optional but enhances visual appeal. Raw cacao powder offers more antioxidants than chocolate protein powder.

- USDA entries used: whey isolate protein powder (1 scoop, 28.7 g): NDB 14058 (Beverages, Whey protein powder isolate); Greek yogurt (nonfat, 227 g): NDB 01256 (Yogurt, Greek, plain, nonfat); cacao powder: NDB 19165 (Cocoa, dry powder, unsweetened); instant coffee: NDB 14214 (Beverages, coffee, instant, regular, powder); honey: NDB 19296 (Honey); granola: NDB 08037 (Cereals ready-to-eat, granola, homemade); blueberries: NDB 09050 (Blueberries, raw).
- Protein powders vary widely by brand. The USDA whey isolate entry is higher in carbohydrate than many commercial isolates, so check your label.
- Granola uses the USDA homemade granola entry. Store-bought granola varies by brand.
