# High-Protein Berry Overnight Oats

No added sugar. Balanced protein, fiber, healthy fats, and micronutrients in one jar.

**Servings:** 1 | **Prep:** 5 minutes | **Chill:** 6+ hours (overnight)

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup (180 ml) unsweetened soy or dairy milk
- 30 g (1 scoop) vanilla or unflavored protein powder (low/no sugar)
- 1/2 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- Pinch of salt
- 1/2 cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
- 1 tbsp chopped walnuts

## Instructions

1. **Blend the protein.** In a jar, whisk the protein powder into the milk until it's completely smooth. Doing this first prevents clumps.
2. **Add the base.** Stir in the oats, Greek yogurt, chia seeds, flaxseed, cinnamon, and salt until evenly combined.
3. **Add berries.** Fold in the berries, or layer them on top. Frozen berries thaw overnight and release natural sweetness.
4. **Chill overnight.** Seal and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, or overnight.
5. **Top and serve.** Stir, add a splash of milk if it's too thick, and top with the walnuts just before eating so they stay crunchy.

## Approximate Nutrition (per serving)

| Nutrient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 520–560 kcal |
| Protein | 40–45 g |
| Fiber | 13–15 g |
| Total sugar | ~10 g (no added sugar) |

All of the sugar comes from the berries, milk, and yogurt.

## Nutrition Highlights

- **Omega-3s:** from the flax, chia, and walnuts
- **Vitamin C and antioxidants:** from the berries
- **Calcium:** from the soy or dairy milk and the Greek yogurt
- **Slow-digesting carbs:** from the rolled oats

## Tips

- **Choosing a protein powder:** Look for 2 g of sugar or less per scoop. Whey or casein gives the creamiest texture, and casein thickens it the most. Pea or soy protein works well for a plant-based version.
- **Plant-based swap:** Use soy milk, pea or soy protein, and an unsweetened plant yogurt.
- **More sweetness without sugar:** Mash in half a small banana, or add a few drops of stevia or monk fruit.
- **Meal prep:** Keeps 3–4 days in the fridge, so you can make several jars at once.
