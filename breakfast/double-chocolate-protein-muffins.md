# Double Chocolate Protein Muffins

Rich, chocolatey one-bowl muffins made with just 6 ingredients. Protein pancake mix and protein powder keep them lower in carbs, calories, and fat than a typical double chocolate muffin.

Source: [Lauren Fit Foodie](https://laurenfitfoodie.com/double-chocolate-chip-protein-muffins/)

**Servings:** 12 muffins
**Prep time:** 5 minutes
**Cook time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (212 g) dark chocolate protein pancake mix (such as Kodiak)
- ¼ cup (30 g) vanilla protein powder (or chocolate protein powder)
- 1 tbsp (5 g) dark cocoa powder
- 1 cup (244 g) canned pumpkin
- 1 cup (240 g) milk (almond milk works well)
- 2 tbsp (30 g) mini chocolate chips, plus extra for topping if desired

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a muffin pan with nonstick spray.
2. Add the pancake mix, protein powder, cocoa powder, pumpkin, and milk to a bowl and mix to combine.
3. Fold in the chocolate chips.
4. Divide the batter among 12 muffin cups. Top with extra chocolate chips if desired.
5. Bake for 18–20 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool for 5–10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Vegetarian.
- For meal prep, mix all the chocolate chips into the batter instead of adding extra on top. The muffins are less messy to eat on the go.
- Store in an airtight container in the fridge for 6–7 days, or freeze in a zip-top bag with the air pressed out for up to 4 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge or on the counter for 10–20 minutes.
