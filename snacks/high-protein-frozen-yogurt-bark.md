# High Protein Frozen Yogurt Bark

A perfect make-ahead afternoon snack packed with protein from Greek yogurt and protein powder. Mixed berries and granola add extra flavor and crunch.

Source: [All the Healthy Things](https://allthehealthythings.com/frozen-yogurt-bark/)

**Servings:** 12 pieces
**Freeze time:** at least 3–4 hours

## Ingredients

- 2 cups plain whole milk Greek yogurt
- 2–4 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 scoop plain or vanilla protein powder
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ cup strawberries, diced
- ½ cup blueberries
- ½ cup granola

## Instructions

1. Line a quarter sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Combine the Greek yogurt, maple syrup, protein powder, and vanilla extract in a bowl; mix thoroughly.
3. Spread the yogurt mixture evenly on the prepared pan, then scatter the strawberries, blueberries, and granola on top.
4. Freeze until very firm, at least 3–4 hours.
5. Cut with a knife or break into pieces by hand.

- Cholesterol: 5 mg

**Sources and assumptions:**

- Nutrient values come from the USDA National Nutrient Database for Standard Reference, Legacy Release (SR Legacy). It is available in [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/), where each entry can be found by its NDB number. Quantities were converted to grams using USDA portion weights where available.
- USDA entries used: whole milk Greek yogurt: NDB 01293 (Yogurt, Greek, plain, whole milk); maple syrup (3 tbsp): NDB 19353 (Syrups, maple); protein powder (1 scoop, 30 g): NDB 14066 (Beverages, Protein powder whey based); vanilla extract: NDB 02050 (Vanilla extract); strawberries: NDB 09316 (Strawberries, raw); blueberries: NDB 09050 (Blueberries, raw); granola: NDB 08037 (Cereals ready-to-eat, granola, homemade).
- Uses 3 tbsp maple syrup, the middle of the 2–4 tbsp range. Each tablespoon adds or removes about 4 calories and 1 g sugar per piece.
- Granola uses the USDA homemade granola entry. Store-bought granola varies by brand.

## Notes

- Vegetarian.
- Store leftovers in the freezer for up to 3 months.
