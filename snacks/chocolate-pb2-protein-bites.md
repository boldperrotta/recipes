# Chocolate PB2 Protein Bites

A higher-protein, lower-fat version of chocolate peanut butter protein balls. Powdered peanut butter stands in for regular peanut butter, and extra chocolate protein powder brings each bite close to 10 g protein per 100 calories. No baking, one bowl, and they keep all week in the fridge.

**Servings:** 10 bites
**Prep time:** 10 minutes, plus 15 minutes chilling

## Ingredients

- 3 scoops (about 90 g) chocolate whey protein powder
- ½ cup powdered peanut butter (such as PB2)
- ½ cup old-fashioned rolled oats
- 2 tbsp honey (or sugar-free syrup)
- ⅓ cup 2% milk, added a little at a time
- Optional: 1 tbsp mini chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Stir the protein powder, powdered peanut butter, and oats together in a medium bowl until there are no clumps.
2. Add the honey, then add the milk 1 tablespoon at a time, mixing well after each addition. Stop once the dough is soft enough to roll but not sticky. You may not need all the milk.
3. If the dough gets too wet, add a spoonful of protein powder or oats.
4. Fold in the chocolate chips, if using.
5. Chill the bowl for about 15 minutes, then roll into 10 balls.
6. Transfer to an airtight container.

## Nutrition (per serving)

Per serving (1 bite, 1/10 of the recipe). Calculated from USDA data for each ingredient (see sources below).

- Calories: 80
- Protein: 10 g
- Total fat: 1 g
- Saturated fat: less than 0.5 g
- Carbohydrates: 9 g
- Fiber: 1 g
- Sugars: 5 g
- Sodium: 25 mg
- Cholesterol: less than 5 mg

**Sources and assumptions:**

- Nutrient values come from the USDA National Nutrient Database for Standard Reference, Legacy Release (SR Legacy). It is available in [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/), where each entry can be found by its NDB number. Quantities were converted to grams using USDA portion weights where available.
- USDA entries used: protein powder (90 g): NDB 14066 (Beverages, Protein powder whey based); powdered peanut butter (48 g): NDB 16091 (Peanut flour, defatted); rolled oats (40.5 g): NDB 08120 (Cereals, oats, regular and quick, not fortified, dry); honey (42 g): NDB 19296 (Honey); 2% milk (81 g): NDB 01079 (Milk, reduced fat, fluid, 2% milkfat).
- USDA has no entry for branded powdered peanut butter, so defatted peanut flour is used as the closest match. PB2 keeps a little more fat. Using its label values adds about 4 calories and 0.5 g fat per bite.
- The USDA protein powder entry is unflavored whey. Chocolate protein powders vary by brand (typically 110–150 calories per scoop), so check your label.
- Uses all ⅓ cup of milk. The optional chocolate chips are not included and add about 5 calories per bite.
- Swapping the honey for sugar-free syrup removes about 13 calories and 3.5 g sugar per bite.

## Notes

- Vegetarian. Gluten-free if you use certified gluten-free oats.
- Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week, or freeze for up to 3 months.
- Whey makes the softest dough. Casein or plant-based powders absorb more liquid, so you may need a little extra milk.
- Roll the bites in a little cocoa powder or extra powdered peanut butter so they don't stick together in the container.
