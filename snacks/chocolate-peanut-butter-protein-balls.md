# Chocolate Peanut Butter Protein Balls

A no-bake, one-bowl snack that uses chocolate protein powder. They take about 15 minutes to make, hold up in the fridge all week, and taste like a peanut butter cup.

**Servings:** 16 balls (about 1½ tbsp each)
**Prep time:** 15 minutes, plus 20 minutes chilling

## Ingredients

- 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
- 2 scoops (about 60 g) chocolate protein powder
- ½ cup natural creamy peanut butter (or almond butter)
- ⅓ cup honey (or maple syrup)
- 2 tbsp mini semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- 1–3 tbsp milk, only if the mixture is too dry

## Instructions

1. Stir the oats, protein powder, and salt together in a medium bowl.
2. Add the peanut butter, honey, and vanilla. Mix until everything is evenly combined. The dough should feel like stiff cookie dough.
3. If the dough is crumbly, add milk 1 tablespoon at a time until it holds together when squeezed. If it is too sticky, add a little more oats or protein powder.
4. Fold in the chocolate chips.
5. Chill the bowl for 15–20 minutes so the dough is easier to handle.
6. Roll into 16 balls, about 1½ tablespoons each, and set them on a parchment-lined plate or tray.
7. Transfer to an airtight container.

## Notes

- Vegetarian. Gluten-free if you use certified gluten-free oats.
- Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week, or freeze for up to 3 months. Eat frozen balls straight from the freezer or thaw for 10 minutes.
- Whey powder makes a softer dough. Casein or plant-based powders absorb more liquid, so you will likely need the extra milk.
- Variations: roll the balls in shredded coconut or cocoa powder, add 2 tbsp ground flaxseed, or swap the chocolate chips for chopped peanuts.
