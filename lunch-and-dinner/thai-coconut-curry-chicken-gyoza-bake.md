# Thai Coconut Curry Chicken Gyoza Bake

A quick, flavor-packed Trader Joe's dinner with just a few ingredients.

**Servings:** 4
**Prep time:** 5 minutes
**Bake time:** 35 minutes
**Oven:** 450°F

## Ingredients

- 1 bag Trader Joe's Frozen Chicken Gyoza Potstickers
- 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk, preferably full-fat
- 3 tablespoons Trader Joe's Soyaki Sauce
- 1/2 bottle Trader Joe's Thai Style Red Curry Sauce
- 1/2 tablespoon minced garlic (1 1/2 teaspoons)
- 2 generous handfuls baby spinach
- Trader Joe's Crunchy Chili Onion, to taste
- 2-3 green onions, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.
2. In a bowl, whisk together the coconut milk, Soyaki sauce, red curry sauce, and minced garlic until smooth.
3. Arrange the frozen chicken gyoza in a single layer in a baking dish. Scatter the baby spinach around and between the dumplings.
4. Pour the coconut curry mixture evenly over the dumplings and spinach.
5. Bake for about 35 minutes, until the sauce is bubbling, the dumplings are cooked through, and the tops are lightly golden.
6. Let cool for 5 minutes. Sprinkle with sliced green onions and drizzle with Crunchy Chili Onion to taste.

**Serving suggestion:** Delicious on its own or over jasmine rice to soak up the coconut curry sauce.
