# High-Protein Mac and Cheese

Creamy stovetop mac made with protein pasta and a blended cottage cheese and Greek yogurt sauce.

**Serves:** 4 · **Protein:** ~35 g per serving · **Time:** ~20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 12 oz (340 g) high-protein pasta (elbows or shells)
- 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
- ½ cup milk
- 6 oz (170 g) sharp cheddar, shredded
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp dry mustard powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

## Instructions

1. **Boil the pasta.** Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until just al dente, following the package time. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
2. **Blend the sauce.** While the pasta cooks, blend the cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, milk, garlic powder, mustard powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper until completely smooth.
3. **Combine over low heat.** Return the drained pasta to the pot over low heat. Pour in the blended sauce and stir in the cheddar a handful at a time until melted and creamy. Keep the heat low so the yogurt doesn't split.
4. **Adjust and serve.** Loosen with a splash of reserved pasta water if it's too thick. Taste for salt and serve right away.

## Notes

- Protein per serving varies by pasta brand, so check the label — some have nearly double others.
- **Protein booster:** stir in 2 cups diced cooked chicken breast (+~15 g per serving).
- Blending the cottage cheese is key — it disappears into the sauce with no curds.
- **Baked version:** top with panko mixed with a little Parmesan and broil 2–3 minutes until golden.
