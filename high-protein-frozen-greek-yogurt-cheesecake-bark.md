# High Protein Frozen Greek Yogurt Cheesecake Bark

A creamy, no-bake frozen dessert with a berry swirl — softer and more cheesecake-like than typical yogurt bark, rather than brittle.

Source: [Fit Men Cook](https://fitmencook.com/recipes/high-protein-frozen-greek-yogurt-cheesecake-bark/)

**Servings:** 10
**Freeze time:** 3–4 hours (plus 2–3 minutes to soften before cutting)

## Ingredients

**Base:**
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 4 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1–2 scoops vanilla whey protein powder
- Pinch of turmeric (for golden color)
- ¼ cup berry compote or jam

**Optional mix-ins:**
- 2 tbsp white chocolate chips
- 1 tbsp cacao nibs
- Chopped nuts
- Granola clusters
- Dried fruit
- Seeds (chia, hemp, etc.)

## Instructions

1. Blend the Greek yogurt, cream cheese, protein powder, and turmeric until smooth.
2. Pour into a parchment-lined 8×8 dish.
3. Drop spoonfuls of berry compote throughout.
4. Create swirl patterns using a toothpick or knife.
5. Sprinkle on optional mix-ins (white chocolate chips, cacao nibs, or other toppings).
6. Freeze 3–4 hours until solid.
7. Let sit 2–3 minutes before cutting.
8. Cut into squares with a sharp knife.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 70
- Protein: 6g
- Fat: 3g
- Carbs: 5g
- Sugar: 4g
- Sodium: 40mg

## Notes

- Vegetarian.
- Stays soft and creamy (cheesecake texture) rather than firm/brittle like traditional bark.
- Store in the freezer up to 1 month in an airtight container, with parchment between layers.
