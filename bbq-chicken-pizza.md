# BBQ Chicken Pizza

A healthier take on BBQ chicken pizza made with whole wheat dough, grilled or baked in a cast iron skillet for a crispy crust.

Source: [Fit Men Cook](https://fitmencook.com/recipes/healthy-bbq-pizza/)

**Servings:** One 12-inch pizza
**Cook time:** 15 minutes
**Total time:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients

- Spray olive oil
- 1 tablespoon almond meal or corn meal
- 3/4 lb wheat pizza dough (store-bought)
- 2/3 cup low calorie BBQ sauce
- 3/4 lb cooked chicken breasts
- 1/3 cup red onion slices
- 3/4 cup reduced fat mozzarella (or pizza blend)
- Pepper, to taste
- Fresh cilantro or green onion, for garnish
- Grated parmesan, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Preheat grill to 400°F or oven to 410°F.
2. Spray a cast iron skillet with olive oil, then wipe dry with a cloth.
3. Dust the inside of the skillet with almond meal or corn meal to prevent sticking.
4. Add the raw wheat dough, spread it out, and thicken the outer crust edges.
5. Layer the BBQ sauce, chicken, red onion, and mozzarella onto the pizza base. Season with pepper.
6. Lightly spray the outer crust with olive oil.
7. Grill (or bake) for about 15 minutes, until the crust is golden brown and crispy.
8. Slice, garnish with cilantro/green onion and parmesan, and serve.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 195
- Protein: 19g
- Fat: 4g
- Carbohydrates: 21g
- Fiber: 2g
- Sugar: 3g
