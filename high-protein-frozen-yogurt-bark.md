# High Protein Frozen Yogurt Bark

A perfect make-ahead afternoon snack packed with protein from Greek yogurt and protein powder. Mixed berries and granola add extra flavor and crunch.

Source: [All the Healthy Things](https://allthehealthythings.com/frozen-yogurt-bark/)

**Servings:** 12 pieces
**Freeze time:** at least 3–4 hours

## Ingredients

- 2 cups plain whole milk Greek yogurt
- 2–4 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 scoop plain or vanilla protein powder
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ cup strawberries, diced
- ½ cup blueberries
- ½ cup granola

## Instructions

1. Line a quarter sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Combine the Greek yogurt, maple syrup, protein powder, and vanilla extract in a bowl; mix thoroughly.
3. Spread the yogurt mixture evenly on the prepared pan, then scatter the strawberries, blueberries, and granola on top.
4. Freeze until very firm, at least 3–4 hours.
5. Cut with a knife or break into pieces by hand.

## Notes

- Vegetarian.
- Store leftovers in the freezer for up to 3 months.
