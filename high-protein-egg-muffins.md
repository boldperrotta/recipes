# High Protein Egg Muffins

Quick, grab-and-go breakfast egg muffins with sausage, spinach, cheddar, and cottage cheese. The blended cottage cheese makes them light and fluffy while boosting the protein content.

Source: [The Almond Eater](https://thealmondeater.com/high-protein-egg-muffins/)

**Servings:** 12 muffins
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Cook time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 40 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 6 oz breakfast sausage (fresh, frozen, or chopped)
- 2 cups spinach, chopped
- ½ cup cottage cheese
- 2 tbsp milk
- 12 eggs
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese, divided
- ¼ cup chives, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F and line a muffin tin with silicone liners.
2. Heat the olive oil in a pan and brown the sausage. Add the spinach during the final minute of cooking, until wilted.
3. Blend the cottage cheese with the milk until smooth.
4. Whisk the eggs with the blended cottage cheese, salt, pepper, ¾ cup of the cheddar cheese, and the chives.
5. Distribute the sausage mixture evenly among the muffin liners. Add the egg mixture on top using a ¼ cup measure, filling cups to the very top for the best puff. Top with the remaining cheddar cheese.
6. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until set and golden. Cool briefly before transferring out of the tin.

It is available in [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/), where each entry can be found by its NDB number. Quantities were converted to grams using USDA portion weights where available.
- USDA entries used: olive oil: NDB 04053 (Oil, olive, salad or cooking); breakfast sausage: NDB 07063 (Pork sausage, link/patty, unprepared); spinach: NDB 11457 (Spinach, raw); cottage cheese: NDB 01012 (Cheese, cottage, creamed, large or small curd); milk (2%): NDB 01079 (Milk, reduced fat, fluid, 2% milkfat, with added vitamin A and vitamin D); eggs (large): NDB 01123 (Egg, whole, raw, fresh); cheddar cheese: NDB 01009 (Cheese, cheddar); chives: NDB 11156 (Chives, raw).
- Uses pork breakfast sausage. Turkey breakfast sausage (NDB 07919) saves about 8 calories and 1 g fat per muffin.
- Salt and pepper to taste are not included.

## Notes

- Store refrigerated for 4–5 days, or frozen for up to 3 months.
