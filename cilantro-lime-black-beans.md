# Cilantro-Lime Black Beans

Saucy, garlicky black beans with a bright hit of lime, made for stuffing into burritos. They're cooked down until thick enough that they won't run out of the tortilla, and partly mashed so they hold together.

- **Serves:** 4 burritos (about 3 cups)
- **Prep time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook time:** 15–20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed (or about 3 cups cooked beans)
- 1 tbsp olive oil or neutral oil
- ½ medium white or yellow onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced (optional; leave the seeds in for more heat)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp dried oregano (Mexican oregano if you have it)
- ¼ tsp smoked paprika
- ¾ cup low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth (or water)
- ½ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1 lime: zest and juice (about 2 tbsp juice)
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 tbsp butter (optional; makes them richer)

## Instructions

1. **Soften the aromatics.** Heat the oil in a medium saucepan or skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and jalapeño and cook, stirring now and then, for 4–5 minutes, until soft and starting to turn golden.
2. **Bloom the spices.** Add the garlic, cumin, chili powder, oregano, and smoked paprika. Stir for about 30 seconds, until fragrant. Don't let the garlic brown.
3. **Simmer.** Add the beans, broth, and salt. Bring to a simmer, then lower the heat and cook uncovered for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until most of the liquid has cooked off.
4. **Mash a little.** Use a potato masher or the back of a spoon to crush roughly a third of the beans. This thickens the beans so they stay put in the burrito. If they get too thick, add a splash of broth or water.
5. **Finish with lime.** Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the lime zest, lime juice, cilantro, and butter if you're using it. Taste, then add more salt or lime as needed.

Add the lime at the end, off the heat. Cooking it dulls the flavor.

Notes:

- Figures assume regular canned beans, drained and rinsed, plus low-sodium broth and ½ tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt. Brands vary, so check your labels.
- Most of the sodium comes from the canned beans. With no-salt-added beans, it drops to about 200 mg per serving. Morton kosher salt is about twice as salty by volume as Diamond Crystal, so if you use it, cut the salt to ¼ tsp.
- Leaving out the jalapeño or swapping broth for water barely changes the numbers.

## Burrito Assembly Ideas

Use warm flour tortillas and layer:

- Cilantro-lime rice
- These black beans
- Grilled chicken, carnitas, or roasted sweet potato
- Pico de gallo or salsa verde
- Shredded cheese (Monterey Jack or cheddar)
- Sour cream or Mexican crema
- Sliced avocado or guacamole
- Pickled red onions

## Flavor Variations

If you want to try something other than lime:

- **Chipotle-orange:** Use orange juice and zest instead of lime, and add 1–2 minced chipotles in adobo with the garlic. Smoky and a little sweet.
- **Salsa verde:** Use ½ cup of jarred salsa verde in place of half the broth. Tangy and herby, and you can skip the jalapeño.
- **Cuban-style:** Add ½ diced green bell pepper with the onion, ½ tsp more cumin, and a bay leaf while it simmers. Finish with 1 tsp red wine vinegar instead of lime.
- **Smoky bacon:** Cook 2 slices of chopped bacon first and use the fat instead of oil. Crumble the bacon back in at the end.

## Storage

- **Fridge:** Up to 4 days in an airtight container. They'll thicken as they cool, so loosen them with a splash of water when reheating.
- **Freezer:** Up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge. Stir in a fresh squeeze of lime after reheating to brighten them back up.
